Documents and local storage
Klik processes documents, images, text and visual signatures on your computer. Imported documents are copied into a local library; drafts, recovery copies and saved signatures remain local. Klik has no document-processing or storage server and does not automatically upload your documents. The library is not an encrypted vault.

You choose when to export, print or share. The selected application, printer, Windows service or cloud folder has its own privacy rules. Windows or OneDrive backups may include folders you choose to back up.

Purchases
No Klik account is required. Windows Pro uses Microsoft Store, including its account, localized prices, payment processing and OS-maintained license. Klik reads available Pro offers and the license status to enable paid tools. Klik does not receive payment-card details. Windows purchases apply to this Microsoft Store application, not the separate Android/iOS stores. RevenueCat is not configured on Windows.

There are no advertising networks, tracking SDKs or custom analytics services in this Windows app. Microsoft may process Store acquisition and diagnostic information under its own privacy notice.

Retention and rights
Local files remain until you remove them. Trash is recoverable; remove exported files and backups separately. The local library may remain after uninstalling. We cannot remotely access or delete your documents. Support email is used to respond to your request; do not send confidential files or payment/identity details. Contact the operator for access, correction or deletion of information sent directly to support, subject to applicable retention obligations. You may contact your data-protection authority.

Windows feature limits
Mobile camera scanning and mobile OCR are unavailable on Windows. PDF-to-Word extracts selectable text and does not preserve the original page layout. Visual signatures are not certificate-based digital signatures.

Microsoft's services follow the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Pro purchases

Klik Pro for Windows

Free features remain available without a purchase. Three successfully completed Pro operations can be tried without a payment card. Cancelled or failed operations do not use a trial operation. Reinstalling or clearing local data can reset the trial counter; it is not a paid license.

Pro is optional. Only offers currently returned by Microsoft Store are available for purchase. The Store shows the local total price and billing period before you confirm. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled through your Microsoft account. A one-time Pro purchase has no recurring charge. It covers the local Pro tools in this Windows app, not future separately priced cloud or AI services.

Windows purchases are linked to your Microsoft Store account and do not transfer to Apple App Store or Google Play. Use Restore purchases while signed in to the same Microsoft Store account. License availability and offline access depend on the OS-maintained Store license. No separate local paid flag is saved by Klik.

Manage subscriptions and applicable cancellation/refund requests through Microsoft Store. Your mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected. Support: szollosi.e11@gmail.com.
